# Preregistration addendum 7: E7, what the free-text constraint parser reads (2026-09-09)

Written before the parser was run on either prompt set and before any label was compared with its output. The book concedes in several places that the parser which decides whether the repair loop fires on a live turn has unmeasured false-trigger and miss rates. This addendum measures both. There are no model calls: the parser is a deterministic function of the prompt text.

## E7. Recall, false triggers and precision of deriveConstraintCheckers

Parser under test: deriveConstraintCheckers in src/organs/instruction-following/checkers.ts of cognitive-middleware at commit 3d20393, the shipped parser, called through the built kit. It emits zero or more labeled checkers of thirteen kinds: exact word count, exact sentence count, forbidden letter, all lowercase, all uppercase, exact comma count, no commas, JSON shape, exact line count, ending phrase, sentence-initial letter, exact word frequency, every word capitalized. By design it declines hedged counts ("at least", "about", "no more than").

Prompt set one, external and labeled: all 541 IFEval prompts (Zhou et al., 2023) with their instruction type lists. A frozen coverage map names the six IFEval types the parser has a kind for: english_lowercase to all lowercase, english_capital to all uppercase, no_comma to no commas, json_format to JSON, end_checker to ending phrase, number_bullet_lists to line count. IFEval's count types (words, sentences, paragraphs, keyword frequency) are stated as comparatives, which the parser declines on purpose, so they are not coverable. Outcomes: recall per covered type (instructions of that type on which the parser emitted the matching kind), pooled recall over covered instructions, prompt-level recall on prompts carrying at least one covered type, and unsupported fires (a kind emitted on a prompt whose instruction list has no type mapped to that kind), with examples. Coverage itself, six types of the twenty-five in the set, is reported as a design fact.

Prompt set two, natural: 300 instructions drawn by a seeded shuffle (seed 20260909) from the public Dolly 15k set (categories open_qa, general_qa, brainstorming, creative_writing, classification; instruction field only, 20 to 400 characters), fixed in dolly-sample-300.json before labeling. Each was labeled by hand, by the author's assistant, before the parser ran on them: yes if the instruction states an exact, machine-checkable format rule of one of the thirteen kinds (with the kind named), no otherwise; hedged counts and content requirements are no. Labels are fixed in dolly-labels-300.json and hashed in FREEZE-7.txt with this plan, the sample and the runner. One labeler is a limit; the labels are published for inspection.

Outcomes on the natural set: false-trigger rate, the share of prompts labeled no on which the parser emitted any checker; recall on prompts labeled yes, a fire of the labeled kind; precision of fires, the share of emitted checkers whose kind matches a label on their prompt. Rates carry 95 percent Wilson intervals. Every false trigger, miss and wrong-kind fire is listed.

Reporting rules written now: the book states the measured rates in the repair chapter's "Where the checker comes from" passage in place of the sentences that call them unmeasured, and in the evidence appendix beside E4. If the false-trigger rate on the natural set exceeds 5 percent, the book says the parser is not safe to arm unattended and that the live path should log and review its fires before acting on them. If pooled recall on the covered IFEval types is below 80 percent, the book says the loop misses most rules phrased outside the authors' own wording. Either way the book reports the six-of-twenty-five coverage.

Predictions written now: false triggers rare on natural prompts, below 3 percent, since the patterns require specific words; recall on covered IFEval types well below 100 percent, because IFEval phrases casing as "all capital letters" and endings with wording the patterns may not match; recall on natural prompts labeled yes low, since natural requests state counts as "give me five ideas" without the phrases the line-count pattern needs.

## Reporting

E7 is reported in the evidence appendix beside E4 with the frozen rules above, the coverage map, the per-type table, the listed false triggers and misses, and the sample and labels files. P1's sentence naming the parser's rates as missing is replaced by the result.
